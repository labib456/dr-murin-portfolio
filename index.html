About the specialist

Clinical expertise, delivered with human understanding.

Dr. Murin Hossain is a Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist with specialised training in child development and experience caring for both children and adults.

She completed her MBBS from Dinajpur Medical College and MD in Child & Adolescent Psychiatry from Bangladesh Medical University. She is currently working at Shaheed Suhrawardy Medical College Hospital. Her approach brings together careful assessment, clear guidance and family-centred treatment planning.
